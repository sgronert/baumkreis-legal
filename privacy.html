Privacy Policy

Baumkreis – Know your Tree (Android) · Effective 19 October 2026 · Version 1. The German version (Datenschutzerklärung) is authoritative.

Baumkreis needs no account, shows no ads and contains no tracking, no usage analytics and no crash reporting. Your game progress stays on your device. The only connection the app makes on its own loads tree photos from the servers of the Wikimedia Foundation.

2. Which feature processes which data

  • Game progress and settings
    Data: solved trees, revealed photos, coins, streak, settings and timestamps (e.g. when a reminder was last scheduled or a rating was last requested)
    Where and why: only locally in the app storage on your device. Nothing is sent to us or third parties.
  • Tree photos
    Data: IP address, time, requested image file, technical device and client information (user agent)
    Where and why: servers of the Wikimedia Foundation (upload.wikimedia.org), to display the photos. See 4.
  • Reminders (optional)
    Data: time and text of the reminder
    Where and why: local notifications of the operating system, no server. See 5.
  • Rating dialog
    Data: none by us; the dialog and any rating you give are handled by Google Play
    Where and why: Google Play (Google Ireland Limited). See 6.
  • External links
    Data: as with any website visit in your browser
    Where and why: only after you tap them (e.g. the photo's source page on Wikimedia Commons, a licence text). See 7.
  • Email to us
    Data: content of your message and your email address
    Where and why: your email app; on our side the mailbox at Google Gmail. See 8.
  • This website
    Data: IP address, time, page requested, browser information (server logs)
    Where and why: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). No cookies, no tracking. See 8.

What the app does not do

  • No user account, no sign-in, no own server, no cloud sync.
  • No advertising. The app does not read the advertising ID; the corresponding Android permission is explicitly removed.
  • No usage analytics, no tracking, no crash reports sent by us.
  • No access to location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.
  • No purchases: this version offers no in-app purchases. The purchase feature is technically prepared but switched off and does not connect to Google Play Billing. Should purchases be offered later, we will update this policy beforehand.

3. Local storage

Your progress is stored only in the app's local storage. We cannot read it. Android's automatic cloud backup is disabled for the app, so the game starts over on a new device. You can delete the data in the app under Settings → "Fortschritt zurücksetzen" (reset progress), via Android's "Clear app data" or by uninstalling.

4. Tree photos from Wikimedia Commons

All photos come from Wikimedia Commons. Some are bundled with the app; the others are loaded on demand directly from the servers of the Wikimedia Foundation, Inc. (1 Montgomery Street, Suite 1600, San Francisco, CA 94104, USA) over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). For technical reasons the Wikimedia Foundation receives your IP address, the time, the requested file and the user agent (information about the app, operating system and network library; the app puts its name and a developer contact address there, but no data about you). We do not receive this data. Loaded photos are cached on the device.

The Wikimedia Foundation processes this data as an independent controller to deliver the images and protect its servers. Its privacy policy applies: foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy. Processing may take place in the USA.

Legal basis: providing the game you want to use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in showing photos from a free source instead of hosting them ourselves (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Without internet the game keeps working; photos that are not bundled are then not shown.

5. Local reminders

After a few solved trees the app asks once whether it may remind you to keep playing. If you agree, it schedules local notifications through your operating system (e.g. "your next tree is waiting"). There are no push messages from a server. You can revoke the permission at any time in Android's settings.

6. Google Play

You get the app through Google Play (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Download, installation, updates and any rating are subject to Google's terms and privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). The app may occasionally show Google Play's rating dialog; whether it appears and what you enter there is processed by Google. From Google we only receive aggregated statistics (e.g. install counts) and, depending on your Google settings, anonymised technical crash and performance data of the operating system; the app itself sends none of this.

7. External links

Links to photo sources, licence texts, the Play Store page or these legal texts open only after you tap them, in your browser or the Play Store app. The privacy terms of the respective provider apply there. We do not pass on any data about you.

8. This website and email

These legal texts are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular IP address, date and time, page requested and browser information, including in server logs. We receive and store emails via Google Gmail (Google Ireland Limited, with processing also in the USA).

Legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the website securely and in answering your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For transfers to the USA, GitHub and Google rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and additionally on Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Legal bases and retention

Storing your progress on your device is technically necessary for the game (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The other legal bases are stated in the respective sections.

  • Local data: until you reset it, clear the app data or uninstall the app.
  • Data at Wikimedia, Google and GitHub: according to these providers' terms; we do not store it.
  • Emails to us: as long as needed to handle them, then deleted unless a legal retention duty applies.

10. Your rights

Where the legal requirements are met, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). As we receive no data about you other than emails, these rights in practice only concern messages you send us; you delete the data on your device yourself (see 3).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): Where we process data based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation, informally, e.g. by email.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Competent for us: Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland-Pfalz, Hintere Bleiche 34, 55116 Mainz, Germany, datenschutz.rlp.de.
